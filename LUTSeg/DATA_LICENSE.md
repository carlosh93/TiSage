LUTSeg reorganizes the SIMATEC/CO2Wounds source images by patient and time and
adds the expert tissue annotations described in the accompanying paper. The
source images were released under CC BY 4.0 with the original
[CO2Wounds dataset](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/nkw5gx57hw/1). LUTSeg
retains that license for the images and releases its added masks, split files,
and metadata under the same terms.

associated dataset metadata. It does not change the licenses of third-party
software or pretrained model weights used by the accompanying code.

privacy, and institutional requirements. The public release is available at
[ksanchez84/LUTSeg](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ksanchez84/LUTSeg).
